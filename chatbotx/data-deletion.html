ChatbotX — Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2, 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of ChatbotX.

How to Request Deletion

Email chatbotx@apps.automatemystartup.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request for requests related to ChatbotX messaging, comments, replies, or Meta/Instagram interaction data.

What to Include

Include enough information for us to identify the account or integration, such as your connected-platform username or account identifier. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive login credentials.

What We May Delete

Where applicable, a deletion request may cover connected-account identifiers, stored authorization records (OAuth tokens), interaction and conversation records, automation configuration, logs linked to your account, and other personal information we are not required to retain.

What Happens Next

We will review the request, verify the relevant information where reasonably necessary, and delete or anonymize information that we control and are not required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Disconnecting Platform Access

You may also revoke VZZN Studio's access through the account or authorization controls of Meta (Facebook, Instagram). Revoking access may prevent future API access but does not necessarily delete information already stored in our systems; use the process above if you also want us to delete data we control.

Third-Party Platform Data

Deleting information from our systems does not automatically delete information independently held by Meta (Facebook, Instagram). Requests concerning data controlled directly by those platforms must be submitted through their own privacy and account controls.
