Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VZZN Studio ("we", "us", or "our") processes information when you use our websites, tools, automations, social publishing features, and connected-platform integrations.

Platforms and Services Covered

Our services may connect to third-party platforms that you authorize, including Meta services such as Facebook and Instagram, and TikTok. We may use self-hosted software and infrastructure, including social publishing software such as Postiz, to operate these integrations. Postiz is used as infrastructure and is not the operator of VZZN Studio.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features and permissions you authorize, we may process:

  • account and profile identifiers, usernames, display names, and other basic profile information made available by the connected platform;
  • OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, authorization status, and related integration identifiers needed to maintain authorized connections;
  • content you choose to publish or schedule, including captions, titles, hashtags, images, videos, thumbnails, and related metadata;
  • publishing settings and status information, such as privacy or audience settings, scheduled times, platform post identifiers, processing status, and API responses;
  • comments, messages, replies, or interaction data only where a feature you enable requires that information and the relevant platform permission has been granted; and
  • technical information such as logs, error details, request timestamps, and security events needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to connect and authenticate authorized social accounts; schedule, upload, publish, and manage content at your direction; display publishing status; troubleshoot technical issues; provide requested automations; protect our systems; comply with applicable legal obligations and platform requirements; and maintain and improve service reliability.

User Direction and Publishing

We do not publish content to a connected account unless the relevant account has been authorized and the publishing action is initiated, scheduled, configured, or otherwise directed through our service. Where a platform requires final user confirmation or editable publishing settings, those controls remain subject to that platform's requirements.

Third-Party Platforms

Your use of Meta, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and other connected platforms is also governed by those platforms' own terms, privacy policies, developer policies, and account controls. We access platform information only through authorized integrations and permissions granted for the relevant account.

Service Providers and Infrastructure

We may use hosting, storage, database, email, monitoring, and other infrastructure providers where reasonably necessary to operate the service. We do not sell personal information or provide connected-platform data to third parties for unrelated advertising or data-broker purposes.

Data Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account connections, preserve security and operational logs, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or support legitimate business records. Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

Access Tokens and Credentials

Platform access tokens and similar credentials are treated as sensitive information and are used only to operate authorized integrations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect such credentials against unauthorized access or disclosure.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may disconnect a connected social account through the applicable service or platform controls. You may also request deletion of personal information controlled by VZZN Studio. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, legal obligations, or platform requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
