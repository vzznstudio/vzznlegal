Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2, 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of VZZN Studio services. ChatbotX and Postiz are separate services with different purposes and contact addresses.

ChatbotX Requests

ChatbotX is used for authorized messaging and interaction automation on supported Meta services, including Instagram.

For deletion requests related to ChatbotX messaging, comments, replies, or Meta/Instagram interaction data, email chatbotx@apps.automatemystartup.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Postiz Requests

Postiz is used as self-hosted social publishing and scheduling infrastructure for authorized accounts, including Meta services, Instagram, and TikTok.

For deletion requests related to Postiz account connections, OAuth authorization, publishing data, scheduled content, or TikTok/Meta publishing integrations, email postiz@apps.automatemystartup.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

What to Include

Please identify the relevant service (ChatbotX or Postiz) and include enough information for us to identify the account or integration, such as your connected-platform username or account identifier. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive login credentials.

What We May Delete

Where applicable, a deletion request may cover information controlled by VZZN Studio such as connected-account identifiers, stored authorization records, publishing metadata, interaction records, logs linked to your account, and other personal information that we are not required to retain.

What Happens Next

We will review the request, verify the relevant information where reasonably necessary, and delete or anonymize information that we control and are not required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Disconnecting Platform Access

You may also revoke or disconnect VZZN Studio's access through the account or authorization controls provided by the relevant third-party platform. Revoking platform access may prevent future API access but does not necessarily delete information already stored in our systems; use the deletion process above if you also want us to delete data we control.

Third-Party Platform Data

Deleting information from VZZN Studio systems does not automatically delete information independently held by Meta, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or another third-party platform. Requests concerning data controlled directly by those platforms must be submitted through their own privacy and account controls.
