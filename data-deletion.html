Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your interactions with VZZN Studio services, including information processed through connected Instagram or Meta integrations.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to chatbotx@apps.automatemystartup.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or interaction, such as your Instagram username or other account identifier. Do not send passwords or other sensitive login credentials.

What Happens Next

We will review the request, verify the relevant information where reasonably necessary, and delete or anonymize data that we control and are not required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Third-Party Platforms

Deleting information from our systems does not automatically delete information held independently by third-party platforms such as Instagram or Meta. Requests concerning data held directly by those platforms should be submitted through their own privacy and account controls.

Contact

If you have questions about this process, contact chatbotx@apps.automatemystartup.com.
